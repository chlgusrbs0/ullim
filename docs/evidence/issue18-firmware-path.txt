Conclusion limited to this installed build:
The standard waveform step omits forwarding amplitude magnitude to its HAL setter.
This is consistent with the equal-intensity logs and user G/H report; it does not
establish the motor's physical limits or the behavior of other firmware releases.
Restoring a system call is a firmware repair candidate, not a validated app-side fix.
